2. Ask for an example of handling a privacy, governance, or responsible-use concern.
3. Review a short work sample: design a pilot adoption plan for one business team.
4. Use the same structured scorecard and questions applied to other candidates.
5. Do not treat the missing exact title as evidence that the candidate cannot perform the work.

## Suggested onboarding ramp

### First 30 days
- Learn approved tools, policies, and escalation paths.
- Interview pilot-team stakeholders.
- Review existing learning and adoption data.

### Days 31–60
- Map one priority workflow.
- Draft a role-based training pilot.
- Establish baseline adoption and confidence measures.

### Days 61–90
- Launch the pilot with documented human-review controls.
- Measure results and collect feedback.
- Recommend the next workflow and iteration plan.
